Development update · October 28, 2026

Slate: a smoother canvas, stronger recovery

By Iian Kehn

The work since Slate’s October release has focused on the parts you feel while writing: the canvas, editing behavior, and recovering changes after an interruption.

A canvas for notes

Text-only notes now have a continuous writing canvas in the source code. Follow-up fixes align editing offsets and preserve embedded objects in notes. The goal is to make lightweight writing feel natural without losing the richer document structure.

Organization and recovery

Recent commits add start-center organization and version-history controls, alongside fixes intended to preserve pending edits and restored content across recovery. Backup work also includes a compilation fix for the encrypted backup writer and its round-trip test.

Committed, not a new release

These changes are in the repository, but there is no newer published release than Slate 2026.10. They should not be read as features already included in that APK, or as confirmation that encrypted whole-library backup is ready for general use.
